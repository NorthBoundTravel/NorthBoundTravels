McCabe family holiday
Booking

Adventure along Northern Ireland’s iconic coast

Overview

This two week long holiday is tailored perfectly for the McCabe family. Relax at Granny and Granda's house near Lisburn and experience Northern Ireland to the fullest.

  • Duration: 2 weeks
  • Departure: Belfast
  • Accommodation: Granny and Granda's House
  • Includes: Breakfast, lunch, dinner and activites.

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival and settle into Granny and Granda's house.
  • Day 2: Explore Belfast – W5 Interactive Science Centre and Titanic Quarter walk.
  • Day 3: Relax – optional Lisburn Leisureplex swimming.
  • Day 4: Tayto Factory Tour in Tandragee
  • Day 5: Visit Carrickfergus Castle and coastal walk in Whitehead.
  • Day 6: Relax at Granny and Granda's house
  • Day 7: Full-day trip to the Giant’s Causeway with optional stop at Dunluce Castle or Portrush beach.
  • Day 8: Rest and local outing – Antrim Castle Gardens or Lough Neagh shoreline walk.
  • Day 9: Visit Castlewellan Forest Park – Peace Maze, forest trails, and paddleboats.
  • Day 10: Relax - Explore the garden or play games indoors
  • Day 11: Visit Lisburn Leisureplex swimming
  • Day 12: .
  • Day 13: Visit Colin Glen Forest Park.
  • Day 14: Pack and departure.
